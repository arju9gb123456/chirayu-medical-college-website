About Chirayu

Every journey starts with a single step they say, and so did ours. In a small 20-bed hospital in the year 1992, an establishment that would grow into one of Central India's biggest healthcare group was born.

After 5 years of incredible hard work and a distant vision and efforts of our founder Dr. Ajay Goenka, we managed to upgrade into a premier super specialty hospital with 100 beds. Our mission of providing quality healthcare started showing promise.

In 2004, we started a Cardiac centre devoted to the treatment of those in need of critical cardiac care. From then on Chirayu just kept adding new feathers to its hat.........

  1. 1992A small 20-bed hospital
  2. +5 yrsSuper specialty hospital with 100 beds
  3. 2004Cardiac centre for critical cardiac care
  4. TodayChirayu Medical College & Hospital
